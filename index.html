Field-to-Map

Five missions, in order. Total event time: 120 minutes. Mission time budgets are 10, 20, 20, 30, and 40 minutes respectively, with the synthesis-heavy Mission 4 given the largest share of the clock.

Your facilitator assigns one team name at check-in. Select only the name your group was given. The two pools are distinct, but neither is ranked above the other.

Undergraduate pool (14)

Named for the forest itself: species, structure, and the physical landscape.

TeamMeaning
Canopy WatchThe canopy is the top layer of forest - the first thing satellites see, and the first thing to change when clearing starts.
Kaolin RidgeKaolin is the pale clay soil common in the region; exposed kaolin can make a cleared patch show up bright on imagery.
Iroko UnitIroko is a prized West African hardwood, heavily targeted by loggers - one of the trees at the centre of the real story.
Silt LineSilt washing into rivers is a downstream signature of upstream clearing; a visible "silt line" is evidence from above.
Ebony TraceEbony is another high-value tropical hardwood; "trace" means tracking what was taken.
Mahogany TrackMahogany is a major commercial timber species; "track" points to the access roads that lead to it.
Raffia PointRaffia palm grows in wetter parts of the forest - a marker of a different, non-timber forest zone.
Buttress RootThe wide flaring roots of large rainforest trees - a signature of old-growth, which is lost first.
UnderstoryThe layer beneath the canopy - what satellites cannot see directly, a reminder that imagery has limits.
Rattan PathRattan is a climbing palm harvested by local communities - representing legitimate, small-scale forest use.
Hardwood Bearing"Bearing" doubles as a compass direction and as a stand bearing timber.
Afzelia GridAfzelia africana is a protected hardwood species; "grid" names the analysis framework.
Liana CrossLianas are woody vines linking canopy trees - a marker of intact, connected forest.
Clearwater BendA river bend running clear rather than silt-laden - what an undisturbed catchment looks like.

Early career pool (6)

Named for the methodology instead: survey, measurement, and reference terms.

TeamMeaning
Sentinel PassDoubles as the Sentinel satellite program and "sentinel" as a watcher; a "pass" is a satellite's overflight.
Baseline NorthA baseline is your reference state before change - exactly what the reference area does in Mission 2.
Meridian FoldA meridian is a line of longitude; core coordinate-system language.
Transect NineA transect is a standard ecological sampling line - straight from field survey methodology.
Datum LineA datum is the reference surface all coordinates are measured against - nothing is locatable without one.
Reference FrameThe coordinate framework everything else is positioned within.
